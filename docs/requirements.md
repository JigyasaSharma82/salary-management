# Salary Management System - Requirements

## Project goal

Replace the HR team's spreadsheet-based process for managing salary data with a web-based system that supports salary management for approximately 10,000 employees across multiple countries and provides clear insight into how the organisation pays its people.

## Target user

The primary user is the HR Manager, responsible for maintaining employee salary information and using it to understand salary patterns across the organisation.

## In scope

- Maintain employee and salary records, including the information needed to identify an employee, their organisational context, country, salary, and salary currency.
- View, find, and filter employee salary data efficiently across the workforce.
- Add, update, and review salary information through the web application.
- Provide salary insights that help the HR Manager answer questions about how the organisation pays people, including comparisons and summaries by relevant organisational and geographic groupings.
- Support a dataset of approximately 10,000 employees across multiple countries.

## Out of scope

- Payroll processing, tax calculation, payments, and benefits administration: the problem is salary-data management and insight, not running payroll.
- Broader HR workflows such as recruitment, leave, performance reviews, or employee self-service: these are outside the stated HR Manager use case.
- Advanced compensation planning, forecasting, or automated pay recommendations: these add complexity without being required to manage and understand current salary data.
- Complex integration, migration, and workflow automation: the assessment does not require replacing every surrounding HR or spreadsheet process.

## Important product requirements

- The system must be web-based and usable by the HR Manager for everyday salary-data management.
- Salary data must remain understandable across countries by retaining the applicable country and currency.
- Data views and insights must remain responsive and practical at the target workforce size.
- Information shown in lists, filters, and insights must be consistent with the underlying employee salary records.
- The product should prioritise clarity and reliable core workflows over feature breadth.

## Success criteria

- The HR Manager can manage salary records for a workforce of roughly 10,000 employees without relying on Excel for the core workflow.
- The HR Manager can quickly locate and review relevant employee salary data across countries.
- The HR Manager can use the system to obtain clear, trustworthy summaries of how employees are paid across relevant groups.
- The delivered scope directly addresses the assessment problem while avoiding unrelated HR and payroll functionality.
